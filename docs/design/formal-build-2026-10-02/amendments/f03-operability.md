# F03 condition-to-machinery alternatives

**Selected provisional implementation defaults:** the parent selected F02-A supplies/two racks, F03-A plus paid overhaul, four useful utilities, retained timed study and conservative legacy Keep/Abandon/fully paid Start fresh under user authorization. Numerical amounts, masses, durations, XP, rest thresholds and 0.60 earned condition are tunable hypotheses requiring measured survival and all-class route evidence; they are not owner-approved balance or accepted gameplay. Actual descriptor/physical qualification remains required. Refused migration never deletes, overwrites or replaces original legacy bytes; retain them for later recovery.

**Revision 3, F03-A and paid overhaul selected provisionally, not implemented.** Both mount and detach shortcuts are removed: ordinary dismount must not call DamageSubcomponent(link, 1.0), and install/load must not call RestoreSubcomponentOnRemount(..., 0.55). Detach changes link availability while preserving saved machinery health and the component's condition. Existing OnShipModUninstalled/ApplyShipModConsequences damage0.6 and panel/load remount paths must follow the same availability rule. Removal damage is not part of this proposal; adding destructive removal would require a separately quantified explicit action, not an implicit detach tax.

Selected provisional A leaves known component condition unchanged across transfer/install/Continue and caps effective operation. Existing system operational thresholds remain; this amendment does not choose new power/engine efficiency curves. For links with no previously independent machinery damage, initialize from saved exact evidence only; otherwise expose reconciliation requirement, not default health.

## Required same-delivery legacy resolution

Unknown legacy equipment remains condition_state=unknown, condition=null until exact evidence or paid work resolves it. ListInstallTargets returns condition_unknown and offers Inspect/Overhaul from the selected instance in InventoryPanel/ShipModificationPanel. Inspection preserves an exact unambiguous saved witness; it never estimates prior health.

Selected provisional paid compatibility overhaul is a F03/F06 deliverable, not future optional work: acquired wrench, repair 0, scrap_metal x1+wiring_bundle x1, 30 nominal eligible work seconds through WorkEligibility/WorkTransactionState, ordinary stamina/wound/rest rules and zero XP. It requires a carried/open reachable unmounted legacy_unknown instance and a mechanical work surface. A passive surface at maintenance_01 floor_cell_d1_x5_z2, proposed target[20, 4.8, 7.2], standing cell[5, 2, 1], works without electricity or repaired fabricator feed. A supported saved ship can use its validated clear local work surface with the same tools/cost; absence of a valid surface fails explicitly. Ordinary new components cannot use this compatibility action to evade their repair gates.

Successful completion consumes the exact inputs and changes the same instance to known condition 0.60, provenance paid_legacy_overhaul, with a receipt retaining before.condition=null and before.provenance. This is the newly achieved condition after paid rehabilitation, never a claim about its previous condition. Definition/mass/identity/holder remain; machinery health does not change. Final-commit consumption, pause/release, target/owner/tool/material rechecks, explicit Continue resume, output/receipt-once and every atomic fault boundary apply. Interruption loses no inputs, produces no resolution/XP and retains bounded progress for explicit valid resume. Known/result replays cannot pay or heal again.

Preflight inspects current location/independent safe shelter, a reachable surface, tools or reachable finite tool source, raw inputs/recovery racks and the count of unknowns essential to the saved route. No automatic new run, guessed health, resource grant or indefinite converted dead end is allowed. Source loss through later deliberate lethal/exhaustive choices is a survival outcome, not a migration promise to replenish the world.

The selected provisional overhaul cost/duration/result requires measured survival/source evidence and surface qualification before F06 acceptance. A refused conversion preserves the untouched original save for later recovery. Unknown cargo round-trip support alone cannot close that gate.

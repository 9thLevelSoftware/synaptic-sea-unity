# Architectural proposals and open decisions

**All SS-ADR entries are proposals for this review.** They are separate from historical Godot ADR numbers mentioned in source comments. Approved requirements are REQ-01..08 in the master design. An accepted architectural decision does not settle an open product choice.

Selected provisional defaults do not claim owner-approved numerical balance or accepted gameplay. Tune versioned definitions against measured survival/all-class evidence, retaining prior paid receipts/earned condition and preserving every refused legacy save for later recovery. Review questions are captured here for the concrete packet; this stage does not ask again about purpose or solo class policy. Approval of the foundation can preserve OPEN-01..05/09..13 for later. F02/F08 use the selected amendments and must attach physical/source/route witnesses before claiming acceptance; if an option is deferred, exposed launch/content capability must be restricted truthfully.

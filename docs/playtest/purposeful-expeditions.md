# Purposeful expedition generation

## What changes in normal play

New, previously unvisited size-1/size-2 contacts after the first successful expedition use `purposeful_expedition_v2`. Odd seeds select a cargo-exchange hull: a broad central cargo hall, radial service routes, distinct medical/crew/bridge/engineering wings and a maintenance extension. Even seeds retain the parallel service-loop family with its sector and side-pocket variation. These are physically different room graphs and hull silhouettes, not color variants. Cargo-hall dimensions and quarter-turn orientation vary deterministically with the seed.

The new profile composes cargo pallets/crates/carts, medical cots/cabinets, crew berths/lockers and engineering benches/racks/trays into clear perimeter bays. Reserved door, objective and loot approach cells stay clear. Furniture bays are distributed around the available perimeter rather than clustered at the first cells. Generic procedural dressing is replaced in furnished rooms; room lighting, fog, gameplay props and existing hazards remain. Imported furnishings use the existing validated self-authored visual bindings. Beds/cots are simple new primitive compositions, not finished production assets. Furniture remains visual-only under the current source contracts; walkable geometry is still authoritative.

The current gameplay builder still supplies role/variant-sensitive salvage objectives, one ordinary container per non-connective room, and a final reach-goal interaction. This slice does not add extra loot, alter starting class skills or implement a new economy. Encounters and condition/infestation overlays continue through the established pipeline. Purpose-specific gameplay still needs richer authored events and medical/living supply definitions beyond the existing generic containers.

## Compatibility and source files

`ShipBlueprint.GenerationProfile` is saved with the seed. Empty legacy identity and `service_loop_v1` regenerate on their original paths; revisiting an old wreck does not upgrade it. Unknown nonempty versions fail closed. New v2 ships regenerate exact layout JSON from the saved blueprint. The starter hub, first-away contract and size-0 boats stay unchanged.

Implementation: `Core/Procgen/PurposefulExpedition.cs`, `ShipLayoutGenerator.cs`, `ShipGenerator.cs`, `Core/Session/RunSession.Travel.cs`, and `Runtime/Ship/ShipSceneBuilder.cs`. The prior landing, shader, camera, whole-wall cutaway and short-junction repairs remain active. Original generated/purchased assets and saved worlds are preserved.

V2 also saves an explicit outward-facing docking port on an actual outer dock floor cell. A 2-by-2 dock centroid previously put the boat halfway across two wall modules, while the fixed facing ignored rotated hulls. Composite geometry now admits the supported overlap at dedicated docks as well as hub airlocks, retaining interior boundaries, locks and unsupported edges. Legacy/v1 port derivation is retained. On restore, mobile control anchors periodically check a clear standing route from the actual player rather than trusting the host spawn's route or the first pre-carving NavMesh result. Only nearby anchors within their real interaction radius can move; undocking restores authored positions.

Longer exploration also exposed a ship-authority mismatch: the lifeboat's travel capability used the deteriorating home hull to calculate thrust. Propulsion now uses the piloted hull and its systems manager. Home web/hull/atmosphere consequences remain active; a damaged piloted hull still incurs the existing penalty. Milestone A still shares the onboarding power grid and systems between home and boat; this is not a complete independent-vessel energy simulation. The current lifeboat has no configured compartment hull and therefore follows the existing pristine-empty-hull contract. Old saves can need ordinary engine spool-up after recomputation; the acceptance waits within the existing bounded departure window rather than forcing thrust.

## Access

For the supported seed-17 start, the verified second-trip choices are `-1:0:0` (size 1, service loop, 17 rooms) and `-1:-1:1` (size 1, cargo exchange, 11 rooms). Select either new contact after the first-away round trip; cargo does not require visiting service first. These IDs are visible in the scanner rows/details. Other eligible contacts vary by saved world. If a contact was visited in an older build, it retains that older profile instead of upgrading in place.

Use the distinct build listed in the audit. New Run keeps the supported default hub. Complete one first-away visit and return; board the repaired lifeboat and stand near its bridge terminal. Press **Tab**, select a previously unvisited contact with size **1** or **2**, then **Travel**. The next outbound trip can use v2; there is no requirement to complete extraction or take several extra trips. An existing save with a visited wreck can choose a new eligible contact immediately. Old visited ships keep their saved generation profile, so returning to one will not show v2. Hull family is deterministic; different eligible contacts can expose both families.

## Acceptance and evidence limits

The compiled structural plan represents an exterior edge with its room owner plus an empty outside owner. Dock overlap admission counts only actual distinct room owners; the outside sentinel must not protect an exterior wall as an interior boundary. The real cargo hull regression reproduces this case and verifies a physical boat-to-host path. Locks, interior boundaries and unsupported void retain collision.

When an entire boundary's collision is admitted into the overlap, its complete visual assembly is suppressed as well, including high trim whose individual bounds do not touch the floor. Undocking restores the assembly. Partially covered frames retain their existing supported-footprint handling. Portal cells are normalized through the established cell parser so typed `Vec2i` and serialized arrays reserve the same door approaches and resolve the same outward dock direction.

Both hull families are tested as independent normal second-trip choices. The exploratory sequential three-trip soak failed when accumulated radiation and incoming threats exhausted the player's health. Survival assertions remain active; the bounded access journeys do not grant health, inventory or skills, disable hazards, or change class balance. Exhaustive repeated expeditions and recovery pacing remain unverified product work. The legacy away fallback applies radiation throughout ships without authored radiation sources; larger layouts need deliberate spatial hazard/recovery design rather than incidental balance changes in furniture code.

Core coverage compares exact regenerated blueprint identity, legacy/v1 preservation, unique floor ownership, dock connectivity, physical cycles and validated structural plans across both families and sizes. Eight additional odd seeds check actual cargo footprint variation and clear portal cells. Unity scene coverage builds both families with real local meshes, checks every floor's NavMesh path, work/loot approach paths, material availability, furniture footprint containment and the visual-only collider contract. Fixture doors are opened for this geometry check; this is distinct from the normal walking journey.

The Windows normal journey uses Title/New Run, real walking and door interaction, finite onboarding supplies, skill-gated repairs, first-away combat, return, Continue, revisit and normal next-contact selection. It then restores the v2 expedition, explores representative room roles, returns home and saves/Continues again. Actual camera/HUD render-texture captures and bounded live-host frame profiling are separate evidence from native desktop input. No keys were sent to the owner's session and no screenshots were uploaded.

## Remaining generator roadmap

1. **Replace the two shape recipes with a constrained room-graph/space solver.** Current `ExpeditionLayoutEngine` and `PurposefulExpedition.Layout` build explicit rectangles and shared-edge links. Add data-defined hull envelopes, functional adjacency rules, side-branch budgets and routed corridors, then validate actual constructed routes. Acceptance should compare many normalized graphs and silhouettes, route depth/loop counts, no overlaps and reachable required work anchors. Keep this behind another saved profile version.
2. **Room compositions as validated catalogs.** Current v2 role arrays and perimeter-bay selection are a foundation, not a complete interior grammar. Add authored bay groups, local asset bounds/clearance metadata, door-facing orientation and optional subsections such as wards, cargo lanes and work cells. Test mesh-to-wall clearance, visual distinction and target approach cones across custom and purchased modules without stretching them or adding clutter indiscriminately.
3. **Exploration and encounter pacing.** `GameplaySliceBuilder` currently repeats salvage/container placement by room; threat encounters still derive from established marker/variant rules. Add distance/branch-aware points of interest, readable optional risks and reward placement using existing tables before expanding quantities. Require reachable objectives under real door/lock rules and natural return/save acceptance. New medical/living rewards or encounter balance need explicit design values rather than incidental changes in decoration code.
4. **Usable multi-deck hulls.** Do not merely stamp more decks. Compose landing footprints and circulation, verify both-direction transfers, lock states, standing clearance, overhead presentation and saved deck identity, then expose stacked expeditions under a versioned generator.
5. **Measured runtime budgets.** Track live frame intervals, render counts/draw calls, scene-generation time, lights and camera blocker-selection cost with the host, survival, AI and HUD active. Optimize spatial occlusion lookup and repeated mesh/light submission before further budget inflation. Report device-specific evidence rather than infer playability from renderer counts.

The unresolved starter-hub expansion, colony mechanics, starting-class balance and creature asset/provenance decisions do not block these generator stages and are not implemented here. This delivery advances two families and purpose-specific rooms; it does not complete the wider procedural-generation request.
